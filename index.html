The Real Problem

Your business changed.
Your brand structure
didn't move with it.

  • New products or categories keep expanding the portfolio, but the role of the brand is becoming harder to define.
  • New channels change how customers encounter and understand the brand.
  • Different markets begin interpreting the brand differently.
  • Individual growth decisions make sense on their own, but together they begin creating structural tension.
  • The business has moved into a new stage, while the brand logic still belongs to the previous one.
How I Read a Brand

Start with an event.
Examine the tension.

I establish what changed, examine the competing requirements it introduces, and explain what they may mean for the brand. The diagnosis separates what the evidence supports from what still needs to be understood.

Process

Structural diagnosis,
delivered in writing.

The work is written-first and asynchronous by default. You send the relevant materials, I diagnose the structural tension, then return a decision-grade written response with clear options and guardrails. Executive discussion is used when a decision is high-stakes, cross-functional, or still unresolved after the written analysis.

I work with founders and C-level leaders at growth-stage consumer brands whose original structure no longer fully supports the business they have become. The work is most relevant when growth, expansion, or transition creates enough observable signals and recurring decisions to diagnose a structural pattern.

My approach is written-first and asynchronous by default. I diagnose the structural tension beneath observable business signals and return a decision-grade written analysis that clarifies what needs to change, what should remain intact, and where decisions are beginning to work against one another.

There are two diagnostic ways to begin. The Signal Read focuses on one observable business event. The Structural Clarity Brief provides a deeper diagnosis of the recurring structure beneath multiple signals. For continuing clients, Structural Advisory provides ongoing review of material decisions as the business keeps evolving.
